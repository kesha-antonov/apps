Privacy Policy - Falling in Fall - stickers

Falling in Fall is a sticker app for iMessage. It does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

  • No accounts or registration.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No advertising.
  • No network requests - the app works entirely inside Messages on your device.
  • No data is shared with anyone.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page.
